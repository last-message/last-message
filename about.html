GLOBAL HUMAN ARCHIVE

About Last Human Message

What is Last Human Message?

Last Human Message is a simple global experiment built around one idea: connecting strangers through words.

One person discovers a message left by another human. After reading it, they have the opportunity to leave their own message for the next stranger.

Why was it created?

The internet connects billions of people, but many online interactions are built around profiles, followers, likes and algorithms.

Last Human Message takes a different approach. It focuses on a simple human-to-human connection between people who may never meet.

How does it work?

A person unlocks a hidden message. They read the words left by another stranger and can then leave a message for the next human.

The result is a chain of messages connecting people across the world.

The idea behind the project

A message can travel further than the person who wrote it. A stranger somewhere in the world may eventually read words that were written by someone they will never know.

That is the experiment behind Last Human Message.
